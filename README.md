### 👋 About me

I'm an iOS developer working with Swift since 2023. I build apps from the first screen to the App Store release, and I also build web systems and AI automations for small businesses through **[Yamaflare](https://yamaflare.com)**.

- 📱 Two apps on the App Store, built alone: **Sniff Radar** and **Donu Task**
- 🤖 AI in production: streaming chat, WhatsApp agents, natural language to actions
- 🏭 10 years in Japan as a quality inspector in automotive factories, so I care about the details
- 🌎 Portuguese, Spanish, English and Japanese
- 📍 Querétaro, Mexico. Open to remote work and freelance projects
